Explanation

Use the menu on the left to move through the calculator:

Inputs: every value the calculation uses: aircraft, aerodynamics, propulsion, limits, take-off, the two altitudes and the air density table.

Calculations: minimum thrust required, maximum speed, rate of climb, V-n limits and take-off distance for each altitude.

Plots: matplotlib plots of thrust, power, rate of climb, the V-n diagram, take-off trajectory, hodograph and the effect of altitude.

Generate Report: a combined, printable summary.

Method: drag polar CD = CD,0 + k·CL², thrust required TR = qSCD,0 + 2kW²/(ρSV²), thrust available scaled by the density ratio ρ/ρ₀.
